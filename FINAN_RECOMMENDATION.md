# Recommendation for Thomas J. Finan (Oir Working Group)

**Date:** 2026‑09‑17

## Context
- The HAVI Level II grant submitted for the *Encoding Ambiguity* knowledge‑graph project was not funded (decision 2024‑09‑14). The proposal remains valuable, but the loss of funding has shifted the focus from building a full‑scale system to establishing a **sustainable governance and labor structure** that allows the project to continue without requiring Thomas to become an LLM/architecture expert.
- Thomas expressed that the initial AI‑driven “low‑hanging‑fruit” phase succeeded, but progress stalled once the harder, more ambiguous data integration tasks began. He wishes to refocus on leadership, scholarship, and community building.

## What the Working Group Offers
1. **Technical ownership** stays with the SLU Research Computing Group (Patrick Cuba) and the RERUM infrastructure team (Bryan Haberberger). They will maintain the AI pipeline, HPC resources, and the RERUM annotation store.
2. **Finan’s role** becomes *Scholarly Lead*: set research priorities, guide domain‑expert validation, and act as the public face of Oir.
3. **No required LLM up‑skilling** for Finan – all model training, prompt engineering, and inference will be handled by the Technical Leads and a dedicated post‑doc.
4. **First‑year deliverable**: a concrete, ambiguity‑preserving worked example of a contested medieval figure (see Charter Section 7). This demonstrates feasibility and provides a publishable artifact for future funding applications.
5. **Consultation & HPC access**: Finan and his team will receive priority access to SLU’s HPC cluster (via the “nursery” arrangement detailed in `tom-finan-initiative.md`) for any compute‑intensive tasks.
6. **Funding strategy**: The group can operate in‑kind for Year 1; a re‑application to HAVI or other programs (NEH Digital Projects for the Public, Mellon Humanities in Place) can be pursued after the proof‑of‑concept is released.

## Immediate Action Items (by 2026‑10‑15)
- **Sign‑off** on the provisional charter (`charter.md`).
- **Schedule** a virtual kickoff (Week 1) – invite repository partners (DRI, VRTI, Logainm) and SLU stakeholders.
- **Assign** a post‑doctoral fellow (or graduate intern) to implement the contested‑entity prototype.
- **Confirm** HPC allocation with the SLU Center for Digital Humanities (named as the “home” for any compute‑heavy work).

## Why This Structure Re‑Motivates Finan
- **Focus on expertise:** Finan can concentrate on historical scholarship, community leadership, and outreach, which are his strengths.
- **Clear division of labor:** Technical work is taken off his plate; the group handles AI/LLM, platform maintenance, and data ingestion.
- **Visible deliverable:** The contested‑entity prototype provides a tangible result to showcase to funders and the scholarly community.
- **Sustainable governance:** The charter defines roles, decision‑making processes, and a path toward future funding without relying on a single grant.
